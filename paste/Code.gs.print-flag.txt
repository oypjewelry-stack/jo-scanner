Code.gs — the page-1 sticker line. A new JO scan queues two stickers: a jo_card and a jewelry_tag.
The phone sends printJo and printTag (JSON booleans) on the page-1 scan only.
false skips that sticker. true queues it. A missing field still queues, so a phone
that has not loaded this scanner yet keeps today's jo_card and also gets the new tag.
